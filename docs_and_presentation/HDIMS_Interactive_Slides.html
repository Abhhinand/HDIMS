The Real-World Paradox

82 Crore Digital Health Records... But Doctors Still Fly Blind

The Patient Reality at 2:00 AM

An unconscious, 72-year-old rural grandfather is brought into the emergency ward with acute dyspnea and chest pain.

  • ❌ Has no smartphone or active mobile data
  • ❌ Cannot read an OTP or approve consent requests on an app
  • ❌ Critical records (past stent, drug allergies) sit locked & idle in the cloud
The Clinician's Struggle

Doctors are forced to choose between delaying life-saving interventions or administering emergency drugs without knowing allergic risks.

The HDIMS Insight: Today's digital health infrastructure demands the highest digital literacy from the exact patients who are in the greatest clinical danger.
Market & Technology Gap

Why Conventional Systems Break Down

1

Traditional Bedside Monitors

They only sound alarms louder. Clinicians suffer from severe alert fatigue (up to 95% false alarms), leading doctors to mute monitors.

2

Static Hospital EHRs

Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) is rigid: doctors either have perpetual access or no access at all, causing massive data privacy violations.

3

Patient Consent Apps

Smartphone-dependent workflows completely collapse in emergency departments and rural tertiary centers.

The HDIMS Architecture

4 Core Pillars of HDIMS

🩺

1. Real-Time NEWS2 Risk Engine

Calculates clinical deterioration (Tier 1 Green → Tier 2 Amber → Tier 3 Red) across 7 vital parameters.

🔐

2. Dynamic Just-In-Time Access (JIT)

Automatically provisions 45-minute ephemeral access to on-call specialists when risk hits Red; self-revoking upon stabilization.

📜

3. Plain-Language Audit Ledger

Translates access grants into human-readable legal sentences for hospital medico-legal protection and DISHA compliance.

🛡️

4. Zero Patient Operating Burden

No OTPs, no logins, no smartphone needed from the patient. Clinician intelligence layer runs entirely on hospital infrastructure.

How the UI Operates (The Magic Moment)

State A: Baseline Patient (NEWS2: 0)

• Ward Nurse View: Full access to live telemetry and routine medication charts.
• Cardiologist View: Sensitive catheterization archives are blurred & locked to enforce least-privilege data minimization.

State B: The Trigger (SpO2 < 85%)

• Judge moves slider or clicks "Acute Desaturation" preset.
• NEWS2 score jumps to 8 (Critical).
• Cardiologist View INSTANTLY UNBLURS with a 45:00 countdown timer, revealing past stent specs before the doctor enters the room!
